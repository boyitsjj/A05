Aaron Sorkin

Aaron Sorkin is an American screenwriter, playwright, and filmmaker recognized for fast-paced dialogue and stories about people working through challenging decisions.

His work includes the stage play A Few Good Men and screen projects such as The West Wing and The Social Network.
